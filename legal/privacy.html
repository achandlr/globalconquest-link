Ferrule: Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Ferrule is a single-player strategy game. It runs entirely on your device. This policy exists to tell you what it collects, and the honest answer is nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no email address, no name. The game never asks who you are, so it has nothing to store and nothing to lose.

The app does not talk to a server

Ferrule never contacts a server. There is no backend, no API and no login service, and the app reaches the network for nothing at all — everything it loads comes from inside its own bundle on your device: the maps, the rules, and the trained neural network that plays against you. The computer opponent is not a service; it is a file that ships with the game and runs offline.

Analytics and tracking

None. There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no advertising identifier, no tracking pixel. We do not know how many games you have played, which maps you prefer, or whether you win. Nobody is counting.

Your device

We never read your contacts, your photos, your location, your microphone, or your camera. The app requests no permissions at all.

Your data

Your games live on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. There is no copy anywhere else, because a copy was never made.

TestFlight

While the game is in beta it is distributed through Apple TestFlight. Apple collects installation and crash information under Apple's own privacy policy, not ours, and we see only what Apple shows any developer: anonymous crash reports and tester counts. That is Apple's collection, and it stops when the beta does.
