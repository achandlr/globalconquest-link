Ferrule: Privacy policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Ferrule is an offline strategy game for playing against computer opponents or sharing a device with other players. This policy explains the information kept on your device and what happens if you choose to contact us.

Your games stay on your device

The game records campaign settings, player names you enter, moves and results in local storage so completed games can be replayed. These records are not sent to a developer server.

An unfinished campaign can be kept while you visit the main menu during the current app session. It is not a cloud save, and the app does not promise to restore an unfinished campaign after it closes.

No accounts, ads or tracking

Ferrule has no account or sign-in service, advertising, analytics SDK, in-app purchases or tracking. Playing a game does not send your player names, moves or results to us.

Maps, rules and computer-opponent models are included with the app. The computer opponents run on your device; they do not send your game to an external AI service.

Keeping and removing local data

Recent completed replays remain on your device. Older recordings are automatically removed when the replay retention or storage limit is reached. Storage limits or an interrupted recording can make a replay unavailable.

Abandon campaign ends the current game; it does not erase all stored replays. There is currently no in-app control to delete individual recordings. To remove Ferrule's local data, use Delete App in your device settings. Offload App keeps app data. Device backups are managed by your device settings and provider, rather than by Ferrule.

If you contact us

Emailing support is optional. We receive the email address and information you choose to include, such as a message or screenshot, and use them to answer your request. Your email provider handles delivery under its own policies.

We keep support correspondence as needed to handle the request and related follow-up. You can contact us to ask about or request deletion of that correspondence. We cannot access or delete game data stored on your device.

Apple and TestFlight

Apple handles App Store and TestFlight installation, diagnostic and feedback information under Apple's policies and your device settings. During beta testing, we may receive the information and feedback Apple makes available to developers. This is separate from an analytics or tracking service inside Ferrule.

Questions about privacy

Contact alex.chandler.memberships@gmail.com with privacy questions. If this policy changes, its updated date and the copy available in Ferrule and on our website will be revised.
