Project Ferrule: Terms of Use

Last updated: August 2026

Project Ferrule is a strategy game distributed as a free beta through Apple TestFlight. By installing it you agree to the following, which is short because the app does very little on your behalf.

It is a beta

The game is unfinished. Rules change between builds, saved games may not survive an update, and features may be added or removed. Beta builds expire — TestFlight will tell you when.

What it costs

Nothing. There are no purchases, no subscriptions, no advertising, and no currency of any kind inside the game.

What it is not

Project Ferrule is an independent game inspired by the classic genre of world-conquest board games. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or licensed from Hasbro, Parker Brothers, or any other publisher, and it uses no publisher's trademarks or logos.

All maps in this game, including the world map, are generated from Natural Earth data, which is in the public domain. Territory names are taken from the real countries and administrative regions each territory contains.

No warranty

The game is provided as-is, without warranty. It is a game; it makes no promises beyond being one.

Feedback

Bug reports and opinions are welcome through TestFlight's feedback button, or by email. Anything you send may be used to improve the game.
