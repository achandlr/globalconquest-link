Ferrule: Contact support

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Found a problem or have a question about Ferrule? You can email the developer. The privacy policy and this support information are available inside Ferrule without an internet connection; sending email needs your email app and a connection.

Email Ferrule support

Help us find the problem

Describe what you were doing and what happened. The map, game settings, your device model and iOS version are useful. Attach a screenshot only if you want to share it.

Opening an email draft does not send anything. Ferrule does not automatically attach player names, a game recording or a screenshot. Please avoid including private information you do not want to share.

Privacy and data requests

Use the same contact for questions about privacy or support correspondence. Game data stays on your device; the Privacy policy explains local retention and removal.
